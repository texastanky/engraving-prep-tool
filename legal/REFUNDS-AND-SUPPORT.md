To be clear and fair, these are outside support:

- **Laser settings and machine configuration.** Power, speed, frequency, passes,
  focus, lens selection, and material recipes depend on your machine and material.
- **Compliance determinations.** We can explain what a marking block field does;
  we cannot tell you what your marking must say, how deep it must be, or whether
  it satisfies any requirement. That is your responsibility under the EULA, and
  it should be confirmed with the relevant authority and your own counsel.
- **Your artwork or third-party files.** We can help with the import behaviour,
  but we do not repair or redraw customer artwork, and we cannot guarantee that
  any particular file (including files exported from other software) will import
  perfectly.
- **General computer, network, or laser-software training.**
- **Machines or operating systems we have not stated we support.**

3. what actually happened, including the exact wording of any message;
4. a screenshot, if the problem is visible;
5. whether it happens every time or only sometimes.

Please **do not** send us serial numbers, customer details, or other sensitive
content unless we ask for it and you are permitted to share it.
