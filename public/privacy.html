Privacy Policy

Engraving Prep Tool

Effective: September 15, 2026

This policy explains what the Engraving Prep Tool (the "Software") collects, where it goes, and what choices you have. It covers the desktop application, our web application, and the account and licensing service behind them (together, the "Service"). We are Petes Guns and Stuff LLC, a Texas limited liability company, doing business as ForgeMark Industries ("we").

The short version: your artwork and shop details stay on your computer and are not uploaded. We collect the account and licensing information needed to run the Service, we count usage so plan limits work, and if you use an AI feature, the content of that request is sent to our server and to a third-party AI provider.

1. Information you give us

Account information. To use the Service you create an account. We store your email address, the display name you provide, a timestamped record of account creation and updates, and a hashed password. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (210,000 iterations with a per-account random salt). We never store your password in a form we can read.

Licence information. If you buy a licence, we store the licence key, the associated account, the plan, and the licence expiry date. A licence key issued to you contains an identifier for your account (typically your email address) inside the key itself.

Payment information. Payments are handled by our payment processor. We receive confirmation of payment, the plan purchased, and limited billing details from the processor (for example a billing name, the last four digits of a card, and a transaction identifier). We do not receive or store your full card number.

Support correspondence. If you contact support, we keep the messages and any files you choose to send, so we can resolve the issue and keep a record of it.

2. Information created when you use the Service

Usage counts. We record how many exports you have performed in the current calendar month and how many AI requests you have made in the current day, keyed to your account. These counters make plan limits work. They contain counts only — not what you exported.

Sign-in session. When you sign in, our server sets a session cookie named pgs_session. It is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, expires after 14 days, and contains your account identifier, email, name, and issue/expiry timestamps, signed by our server. It is used only to keep you signed in.

Technical and security data. Our servers and our hosting provider, Vercel, process standard request information — including IP address, timestamps, request paths, and user agent — as part of serving the Service and protecting it from abuse.

Update checks. The application checks a version manifest over the internet at startup. That request tells us (and our hosting provider) your IP address and your installed version. It does not include your artwork, your shop details, or your usage counts.

3. What stays on your computer

The Software is local-first. The following are stored on your device and are not sent to us:

What Where it is stored
Your artwork, designs, imported SVGs, photographs of parts On your device, in the application's own storage
Your shop profile (licensed name, city, state) Local storage on your device
Saved material presets and custom presets Local storage on your device
Your export history flag and getting-started progress Local storage on your device
Your cached licence entitlement Local storage on your device

Uninstalling the desktop application does not automatically delete this local data; it is kept so that reinstalling preserves your settings, and you can remove it yourself (see section 9).

Exports are produced on your device. Export files are generated locally and saved where you choose. We do not receive them.

4. AI features send content to third parties

The AI assistant and AI image features are optional. When you use them:

  1. your request is sent from your device to our server;
  2. our server forwards it to OpenAI using our own API credentials;
  3. the model's response is returned to your device.

What is included in the request:

Feature What is transmitted
AI assistant (chat) The messages you type, plus a summary of your current editor settings (for example material size and units)
AI assistant, when a design or part photo is attached by the feature The attached image data, in addition to the above
AI image composer The prompt you type and any template or reference image you attach

Do not use AI features with content you are not permitted to share. In particular, be careful with photographs of parts, serial numbers, customer information, or anything confidential.

OpenAI processes these requests as our service provider. We do not use your AI requests to train our own models, and we do not sell them.

If you would rather not send content anywhere, do not use the AI features. The rest of the Software works without them.
